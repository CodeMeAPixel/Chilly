# Code of Conduct

Chilly adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, the Discord server and any other project space.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's experience level or background.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sexualised language or imagery are not acceptable.
- Do not share other people's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers through the contact options on the [CodeMeAPixel GitHub profile](https://github.com/CodeMeAPixel). All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful to the community. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
